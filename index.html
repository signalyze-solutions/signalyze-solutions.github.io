About
The strategist behind the methodology.

Edward (Eddie) Taveras

Founder & Principal Consultant, Signalyze Solutions LLC

With a background spanning enterprise-scale data analytics and financial operations, I've spent years translating raw business data into the insights that drive real decisions — managing analytics across 230,000+ assets, building KPI frameworks, designing profitability dashboards, and developing scenario simulations that model the impact of strategic decisions before they're made.

That experience taught me something most consultants miss: executives don't need more data or another dashboard. They need scored, ranked recommendations they can act on today. So I built Signalyze's proprietary evaluation methodology from the ground up — combining financial modeling, what-if analysis, and multi-factor strategic frameworks into a system that delivers clarity, not complexity.

Every engagement is hands-on. I work directly with each client to understand their business context, calibrate the methodology to their operational reality, and deliver recommendations they can actually execute. Bilingual in English and Spanish.
