How It Works
Analyze. Certify. Simplify.
📊

Analyze

Your data flows through the Elenivo Data Network (EDN) — our strategic intelligence layer that detects patterns across your metrics, benchmarks them against real-world decision outcomes, and evaluates across up to 7 dimensions: Performance, Risk, Capital Structure, Customer Health, Talent & Operations, Innovation Pipeline, and Market Timing.

✅

Certify

Every recommendation comes with confidence scores, flip conditions (the data points that would change it), and key drivers—not opinions. Every decision is supported by scored analytics and transparent reasoning.

🚀

Simplify

AI-enhanced executive narratives translate complex analytics into clear, ranked actions your team can execute today. No dashboards. No noise. Just decisions.

SDE Engine
7 Strategic Dimensions

Each tier unlocks different modules, from foundational capacity tracking to full-spectrum market position analysis.

M1
Performance
Capacity & utilization
▼

Evaluates how efficiently your business uses its available capacity — revenue per unit, workforce utilization, and throughput relative to operational ceiling. Surfaces where you're leaving money on the table.

M2
Risk
Tolerance & exposure
▼

Quantifies your exposure across financial, operational, and market risk vectors. Measures concentration risk, volatility sensitivity, and your capacity to absorb downside scenarios.

M3
Capital Structure
Allocation & efficiency
▼

Analyzes how capital is allocated across your business — debt-to-equity positioning, cost of capital, and whether your investment distribution is optimized for your growth stage.

M4
Customer Health
Churn & retention
▼

Measures the strength of your customer base — retention rates, lifetime value trends, acquisition cost efficiency, and early churn signals that indicate revenue risk before it materializes.

M5
Talent & Ops
Efficiency & scaling
▼

Assesses operational scalability and workforce alignment — revenue per employee, process bottlenecks, and whether your team structure supports the next stage of growth.

M6
Innovation
Pipeline & timing
▼

Tracks your innovation pipeline health — R&D investment ratio, time-to-market velocity, and whether your product development cadence positions you ahead of or behind market demand.

M7
Market Timing
Positioning & shifts
▼

Detects competitive positioning shifts and market timing windows — analyzing sector momentum, competitive movement, and whether conditions favor expansion, consolidation, or strategic pivots.
